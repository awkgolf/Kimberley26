# Kalbarri

## Overview

**Region:** Mid West, WA
**Coordinates:** 27°42′S 114°10′E
**Distance from Perth:** ~590 km north
**Access:** North West Coastal Highway then Ajana-Kalbarri Road

## Geology Summary

**Dominant Rock Type(s):** Tumblagooda Sandstone (Silurian)
**Notable Formations:** Murchison River Gorge; Z-Bend; Nature's Window
**Geological Interest:**
- **Tumblagooda Sandstone** — one of the world's richest early land-animal trace fossil sites (Silurian, ~430 Ma)
- Horizontal red and white banded fluvial sandstone — clearly visible in gorge walls
- Trace fossils (ichnofossils) including *Diplichnites*, *Heimdallia* — early arthropod trackways
- Cross-bedding and ripple marks record ancient river and tidal flat environments
- Murchison River has incised ~100 m through horizontal strata — excellent stratigraphic section

## Coastal Character

**Beach Type:** Kalbarri township beach — Chinaman's Beach; sheltered estuary
**Cliff / Headland Features:** Red Bluff — dramatic red Tumblagooda Sandstone sea cliffs
**Wave Exposure:** High on ocean-facing cliffs (Red Bluff, Eagle Gorge)

## Points of Interest

- **Z-Bend Gorge** — deep meander gorge with excellent sandstone stratigraphy
- **Nature's Window** — iconic sandstone arch
- **The Loop** — walking trail through river gorge
- **Red Bluff** — 80 m red sandstone coastal cliffs
- **Eagle Gorge** — coastal gorge, wave-cut platforms
