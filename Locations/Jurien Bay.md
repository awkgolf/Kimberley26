# Jurien Bay

## Overview

**Region:** Turquoise Coast, WA
**Coordinates:** 30°18′S 115°02′E
**Distance from Perth:** ~220 km north
**Access:** Indian Ocean Drive

## Geology Summary

**Dominant Rock Type(s):** Tamala Limestone, Holocene beach sand and dune
**Notable Formations:** Coastal limestone platform; offshore rocky reefs
**Geological Interest:**
- Raised Holocene marine terraces visible along the coast
- Fossil shell beds in calcarenite
- Limestone solution features (karren, small dolines)
- Marine park — good access to subtidal limestone platform

## Coastal Character

**Beach Type:** Sheltered bay; white carbonate sand
**Cliff / Headland Features:** Low limestone headlands north and south of bay
**Wave Exposure:** Low — sheltered by offshore reef system and islands

## Points of Interest

- Jurien Bay Marine Park — limestone reef snorkel
- Coastal limestone road cuts — cross-bedding and fossil horizons
- North Head limestone point
