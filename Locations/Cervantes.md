# Cervantes

## Overview

**Region:** Turquoise Coast, WA
**Coordinates:** 30°30′S 115°03′E
**Distance from Perth:** ~200 km north
**Access:** Indian Ocean Drive; Nambung NP via unsealed road (~17 km south of Cervantes)

## Geology Summary

**Dominant Rock Type(s):** Tamala Limestone
**Notable Formations:** The Pinnacles — dissolution karst spires within Tamala Limestone
**Geological Interest:**
- The Pinnacles (Nambung National Park) are a world-famous karst landform
- Limestone spires formed by differential cementation and subsequent wind erosion exposing harder calcrete cores
- Silcrete and calcrete horizons visible in cross-section
- Ongoing debate on formation mechanism: root-cast calcrete vs. dissolution remnants

## Coastal Character

**Beach Type:** Carbonate sand; Hangover Bay beach
**Cliff / Headland Features:** Low limestone coastal platform
**Wave Exposure:** Moderate open ocean swell

## Points of Interest

- **Pinnacles Desert** — Nambung National Park; karst limestone spires (essential stop)
- **Hangover Bay** — coastal limestone platform and beach exposure
- **Lake Thetis** — thrombolites (living microbial mats); rare modern analogue for Precambrian reef builders
