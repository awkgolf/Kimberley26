# Lancelin

## Overview

**Region:** Turquoise Coast, WA
**Coordinates:** 30°56′S 115°20′E
**Distance from Perth:** ~130 km north
**Access:** Indian Ocean Drive (Route 60)

## Geology Summary

**Dominant Rock Type(s):** Tamala Limestone (aeolianite), Quaternary coastal sand
**Notable Formations:** Tamala Limestone cliffs and offshore reefs; large active aeolian dune fields east of town
**Geological Interest:**
- Excellent exposures of Pleistocene aeolianite (cemented coastal dune limestone)
- Cross-bedding visible in cliff sections recording past dune migration
- Active dune fields provide modern analogue for ancient aeolianite formation
- Calcarenite with shell fragments and rhizoliths (root casts)

## Coastal Character

**Beach Type:** White quartz-carbonate sand
**Cliff / Headland Features:** Low limestone cliffs and rocky reefs
**Wave Exposure:** Moderate — partially sheltered by Lancelin Island

## Points of Interest

- Sand dune field — outstanding cross-bedded aeolianite exposures
- Lancelin Island — limestone platform
- Southern reef — snorkel/dive site showing cemented carbonate seafloor
