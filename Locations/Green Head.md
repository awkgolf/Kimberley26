# Green Head

## Overview

**Region:** Turquoise Coast, WA
**Coordinates:** 30°04′S 114°58′E
**Distance from Perth:** ~270 km north
**Access:** Indian Ocean Drive, then 3 km sealed spur road

## Geology Summary

**Dominant Rock Type(s):** Tamala Limestone
**Notable Formations:** Prominent limestone headland; elevated coastal platform
**Geological Interest:**
- Well-exposed Tamala Limestone with clear aeolian cross-bedding
- Elevated coastal terrace suggesting Pleistocene highstand
- Honeycomb weathering (tafoni) on exposed limestone surfaces
- Rocky intertidal platform with bioeroded karren

## Coastal Character

**Beach Type:** Small sheltered pocket beaches between limestone headlands
**Cliff / Headland Features:** Striking white limestone cliffs and sea stacks
**Wave Exposure:** Moderate to high on exposed headlands

## Points of Interest

- Green Head Cliffs — excellent tafoni and karren weathering features
- Dynamite Bay lookout — coastal limestone platform
- Rocky intertidal zone — bioerosion and solution features
