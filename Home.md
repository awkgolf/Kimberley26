## About This Expedition

This notebook documents a coastal geological survey travelling north from Perth along the Western Australian coastline in August 2026. The primary focus is on:

- Documenting rock formations, outcrops and coastal geomorphology
- Recording daily observations, weather conditions and travel details
- Cataloguing collected rock and sediment samples
- Mapping geological features along the route

### Region Overview

The coast north of Perth traverses several distinct geological provinces:

- **Perth Basin** — Sedimentary sequences including Cretaceous and Jurassic formations visible in coastal cliffs
- **Yilgarn Craton margin** — Ancient Precambrian basement rocks
- **Coastal aeolianite** — Pleistocene dune calcarenite (limestone) forming the Tamala Limestone
- **Quaternary beach and dune systems**
