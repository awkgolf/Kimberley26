## Geological Overview — Coast North of Perth

### Perth Basin (Sedimentary)

The Perth Basin is a north-trending rift basin containing up to 15 km of Permian to Cretaceous sedimentary rocks. Coastal exposures typically show:

- **Tamala Limestone** (Pleistocene) — aeolianite forming cliffs and headlands
- **Safety Bay Sand** (Holocene) — modern beach and dune sediments
- **Cretaceous sandstones and shales** — exposed in some sea cliffs

### Yilgarn Craton

One of the world's oldest cratons (~2.5–3.7 Ga). Granitoid and greenstone belt rocks may be encountered further north.

### Key Minerals to Watch For

- Quartz, feldspar, mica (granitic rocks)
- Calcite / aragonite (limestone and shell hash)
- Iron oxides — hematite, goethite (ferricrete/laterite)
- Heavy minerals in beach sand (ilmenite, zircon, rutile)
