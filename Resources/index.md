# Resources

Reference material, maps and background reading for the Kimberley26 expedition.

---

## Geological Maps

- Geological Survey of Western Australia (GSWA): [https://www.dmp.wa.gov.au/geological-survey](https://www.dmp.wa.gov.au/geological-survey)
- GeoVIEW.WA online portal: [https://geoview.dmp.wa.gov.au](https://geoview.dmp.wa.gov.au)

## Key References

- **Playford, P.E., Cockbain, A.E. & Low, G.H. (1976)** — *Geology of the Perth Basin, Western Australia.* GSWA Bulletin 124.
- **Semeniuk, V. & Searle, D.J. (1985)** — Variability of Holocene deposits along a high-energy coastline, Perth, Western Australia. *Marine Geology* 67: 199–220.
- **Lipar, M. & Webb, J.A. (2014)** — The formation of the Pinnacles, Nambung National Park. *Australian Journal of Earth Sciences* 61: 51–65.
- **Trewin, N.H. & McNamara, K.J. (1994)** — Arthropods invade the land: trace fossils and palaeoenvironments of the Tumblagooda Sandstone. *Transactions of the Royal Society of Edinburgh* 85: 177–210.

## Useful Apps & Tools

- **GSWA GeoVIEW** — geological map viewer
- **Australian Stratigraphic Units Database** — [https://asud.ga.gov.au](https://asud.ga.gov.au)
- **Mindat.org** — mineralogy and locality database
- **RockD** — field geology app (rock identification, GPS logging)
