# Safety and Permissions

## Before visiting

- Check park, marine-park and land-access rules.
- Confirm whether collecting samples or hammering is permitted.
- Check weather, tides, fire warnings, road conditions and closures.
- Carry offline maps, water, first aid, sun protection and a communication plan.

## Sampling

Collect only where authorised and take the minimum material necessary. Record
the permit or land-manager guidance in the relevant journal entry.

## Acid testing

Only use dilute hydrochloric acid with suitable eye, skin and ventilation
protection. Carry it securely, avoid contact with skin and carbonate surfaces
that should not be damaged, and follow local disposal requirements.
